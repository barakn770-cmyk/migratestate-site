The **0.3%** building rate applies only when an individual buys a residential house to live in (at least 50 m², registered within one year) through **31 March 2027**.   - **buying-property-panama.html** — Decreto Ejecutivo No. 17 of 8 September 2026 (Gaceta Oficial No. 30613, 16 September 2026) replaced the "US$300,000 rising to US$500,000 after October 15, 2026" line.
